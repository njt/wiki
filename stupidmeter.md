# stupidmeter

An AI model benchmarking tool with a deliberately retro UI. Ranks models by their "stupid level" -- presumably testing for common failure modes, logical errors, and hallucination patterns. The retro design aesthetic is the hook.

The approach of measuring stupidity rather than intelligence is a useful inversion. Most benchmarks measure what models can do; measuring what they get wrong is arguably more useful for practical deployment decisions. The framing connects to the eval philosophy in [[Demystifying Evals for AI Agents]] -- testing for failure modes, not just capabilities.

## Critical Analysis

Without being able to fetch the full content, it's hard to assess the methodology. The "retro UI is briefly cool again" annotation suggests this is as much a design statement as a serious benchmarking tool. The site likely joins a crowded field of model comparison tools, but the framing (stupid level rather than smart level) and the aesthetic distinguish it.

The real question with any model ranking tool: how quickly does it update when new models ship, and how reproducible are the benchmarks? Most model ranking sites become stale within weeks of launching.
