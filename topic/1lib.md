# 1lib

A digital library and book search engine, similar to Anna's Archive. Provides access to books and academic papers.

---

## Key Themes

#books #library #access

Not much to say beyond the annotation: it's a shadow library in the tradition of Library Genesis and Anna's Archive. These sites exist in a legal grey area but serve a genuine need for access to knowledge, particularly in countries where academic papers and textbooks are prohibitively expensive.

## Critical Analysis

The site was unreachable when fetched (ECONNREFUSED), which is typical for shadow libraries -- they move domains frequently and are often under legal pressure. The ephemeral nature of the infrastructure is part of the design.

Whether you consider these services piracy or access-to-knowledge activism depends on your politics. Either way, they exist and they're used.
