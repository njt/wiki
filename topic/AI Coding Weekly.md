# AI Coding Weekly

A weekly curated digest from Data Drift Press covering the most significant AI development news. Focuses on practical tools, libraries, techniques, and industry trends rather than pure research.

---

## Key Themes

#feed #news #ai-coding #developer-tools

A focused newsletter that tracks the AI coding tools ecosystem week by week. The coverage skews practical -- model comparisons, security vulnerabilities in AI-generated code, adoption surveys, tool releases. Less "here's a new paper" and more "here's what shipped and what broke."

Notable data points from recent issues: Claude Code hit #1 among surveyed engineers in just eight months (Issue #46), 140K+ vulnerabilities found in coding skills (Issue #49), Mozilla squashed 423 bugs in one month with AI assistance (Issue #54). These are the kind of concrete metrics that cut through the hype cycle.

## Critical Analysis

The value is in the curation and the weekly cadence. The AI coding space moves fast enough that daily coverage is overwhelming and monthly coverage is stale. Weekly is the right rhythm for practitioners who need to stay current without doom-scrolling.

Pairs well with [[Spicy Takes Feed]] for commentary and opinion. AI Coding Weekly gives you the facts; Spicy Takes gives you the arguments about what those facts mean.
