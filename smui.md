# smui

A terminal-aesthetic theme for shadcn/ui. Nord-inspired palette, sharp edges (border-radius: 0rem everywhere), monospace everything (JetBrains Mono). Inspired by the starship terminals of SpaceMolt. It makes your React app look like it belongs on a spacecraft bridge.

## Key Themes

#design #ui #theme #shadcn

The design system is disciplined: four frost blues, five aurora status colors, all exposed as CSS variables. Four surface depth levels for hierarchy without rounded corners. Light (Snow Storm) and dark (Polar Night) modes. It's a complete design language, not just a color swap.

The uppercase labels with 1.5px letter-spacing are a nice touch -- they evoke the mechanical, utilitarian feel of actual terminal interfaces without sacrificing readability. JetBrains Mono was a good choice; it's one of the few monospace fonts designed for extended reading rather than just code.

## Critical Analysis

Terminal aesthetics in web apps are having a moment, and smui is one of the better executions. The sharp-edge-everything approach is a genuine design choice rather than laziness -- it creates visual coherence that rounded-corner-everything can't match.

The practical question is longevity. Terminal themes are briefly cool, then feel dated. But if you're building internal tools, developer dashboards, or anything where the audience appreciates the aesthetic, this is a strong foundation. The shadcn/ui integration means it drops into any Next.js or React project with minimal friction.
