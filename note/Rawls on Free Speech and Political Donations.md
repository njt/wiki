## Opinionated take

The post is thin — one recommendation and one observation — but the observation is sharp. The "free speech" framing of political donations is exactly the kind of single-principle lens that flattens a structural question (who gets effective voice in a democracy) into an absolute (speech cannot be restricted), and Rawls's own ordering — basic liberties yes, but with the *fair value* of political liberty protected against money — is one of the few mainstream philosophical tools that cuts against it. Davie's "If only, eh." concedes the real point: having the right framework changes nothing about a lobbying fight already in progress. The post's value to this wiki is as an example of philosophy being used as a live lens on local politics rather than as heritage — a small but genuine counterpoint to the wiki's technology-heavy diet.
