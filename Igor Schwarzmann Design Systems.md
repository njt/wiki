# Igor Schwarzmann Design Systems

A now-defunct personal reference site by Igor Schwarzmann that catalogued design systems. The site is gone; what remains worth noting is who built it and why.

---

Igor Schwarzmann is a German design strategist who co-founded Third Wave, a futures-thinking consultancy, and later became Senior Director for Strategic Design at Edenspiekermann. His work sits at the intersection of foresight, strategy, and digital product design — a lens that frames design systems not as component libraries but as organizational operating systems.

The site `designsystems.igorschwarzmann.com` (now dead, no archives) was his curated reference. Given Schwarzmann's background, it likely treated design systems as strategic artifacts: how they encode organizational decisions, how they scale design across teams, and how they evolve under constraints.

> "Design systems are not about consistency. They're about distribution of decision-making." — this is the kind of take Schwarzmann's work implies, even if we can't quote the site directly.

## Critical Analysis

The site's disappearance is unsurprising. Personal curated lists have a half-life of about three years — the maintainer moves on, the domain expires, and the content evaporates. This is the same pattern that makes GitHub awesome-lists unreliable over time: curation is maintenance, and maintenance is expensive.

What's more interesting is the **who** rather than the **what**. Schwarzmann wasn't a frontend engineer cataloguing button components. He was a strategic designer who treated design systems as a lens on organisational decision-making. That framing is more durable than any specific list of design systems.

The loss of the site is a small but real example of link rot in the design-tooling canon. If Schwarzmann's commentary was good, it's now gone — another argument for personal archiving of reference material.
