# The Happiest I've Ever Been
By Ben Wallace

## Main Narrative
Ben Wallace describes his experience as a youth basketball coach in early 2020, which he identifies as the happiest period of his life. After feeling empty in his first post-college job, he volunteered as an assistant coach for a middle school team -- a role that unexpectedly became a head coaching position. His team of six students went undefeated after their first loss.

## Why He Found Happiness
Four essential elements:
1. Meaningful work with young people
2. Physical, tangible engagement -- remote alternatives would have lacked the essential in-person elements
3. Agency and leadership -- making real decisions about practice, strategy, player development
4. Alignment with personal passion

## Notable Quotes

"I fucking loved being a coach. And, I don't want to brag, but I was really good at it."

"I love being in the real world. If I taught those kids on Zoom... it wouldn't have been the same."

## Broader Message
Connects to contemporary tech industry anxieties. "For years, you've sat in front of a rectangle, moving tinier rectangles, only to learn that AI can now move those rectangles 10x better. As someone outside the equity class, you begin to wonder what your role is in this new paradigm. And whether rectangles were ever your ticket to happiness in the first place."

Happiness requires alignment between personal values, actual abilities, and meaningful impact -- not scalable products.
