# Scour

A personalized content feed that searches thousands of sources to surface exactly what you want to read but might miss otherwise.

## What It Is

Scour positions itself as an alternative to noisy feeds you can't keep up with and a way to discover smaller sites you didn't know to check.

## Site Statistics (as of fetch date)

- Posts scoured this month: 823,198
- Community-added sources: 28,161
- Hidden gems surfaced this month: 65,122
- 3,317 readers joined

## How It Works

1. **Add your interests** — Free-form text, "as broad or niche as you want." Scour uses semantic matching to find relevant articles.
2. **Search everywhere** — Scour covers "the HN fire hose, Reddit, arXiv, and thousands of blogs and newsletters." Users can curate sources or search them all.
3. **Discover hidden gems** — Content you wouldn't find otherwise. Ranking is relevance-based: "A post with 1 upvote can rank above one with 1,000 if it's more relevant to you."

## Key Features

- Interest-based semantic matching with free-form descriptions
- Curated or full-source feeds (HN, Reddit, arXiv, blogs, newsletters)
- Relevance-based ranking that favors hidden gems over raw popularity
- Weekly email digest ("Friday scour emails")
- Live feed sharing (Evan's feed is publicly viewable)
- Keyboard shortcuts for full navigation:
  - Navigation: j/k (next/previous post), o or Enter (open), v (preview)
  - Post actions: a (love), l (like), d (dislike), u (undo), s (save/unsave)
  - Recommendations: Enter (add interest/feed), x (not interested)
  - Go to: gh (home), gi (interests), gf (feeds), gl (likes), gy (history), gc (changelog), gs (settings), gb (discover), / (search)
  - Pagination: n (next page), p (previous page)
  - General: ? (help), ! (submit feedback), Esc (close/unfocus)
