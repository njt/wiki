    # Title:Estimating $π$ with a Coin

View PDF HTML (experimental)Abstract:We describe a simple Monte Carlo method for estimating $\pi$ by tossing a coin. Although the underlying Catalan-number series identities appear implicitly in the probability theory literature, the interpretation of $\frac{\pi}{4}$ presented here seems to be new.
