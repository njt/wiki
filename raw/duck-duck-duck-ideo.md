# Duck, Duck, Duck! by IDEO

A product/art project under IDEO's Edges initiative — a playful rubber duck available in three tiers: free open-source software (GitHub), DIY hardware (firmware, PCB, CAD files), and a waitlist for an official physical version.

## The Flock — Four Colorways

**Pekin** — "The understated one"
All-white with a soft yellow beak. "He doesn't need the spotlight, just a place beside you."

**Mallard** — "The wild one"
The self-described "bad boy" with "an arrest record as long as a pond is wide. It's mostly bar fights." Offers "scathing judgments about your spaghetti code."

**Pintail** — "The mysterious one"
Black and grey with a beak piercing. "Zero tolerance for bird-based BS." Wants "to get work done with zero pretense."

**Classic** — "The frontman"
Yellow body, orange beak — "the duck to end all ducks." Called "the heart breaker and the myth maker," a "virtuoso on the keyboard."

## Distribution

1. **Software Version** — Free, open source. Full source on GitHub ([github.com/ideo/Rubber-Duck](https://github.com/ideo/Rubber-Duck))
2. **DIY Hardware** — Firmware, PCB files, 3D print files on GitHub
3. **Notify Me** — Waitlist for the official physical version (name + email + privacy consent)
