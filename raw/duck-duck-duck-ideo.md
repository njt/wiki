The flock

He's the sensitive one. He'll sit and listen to you, quiet and caring, after a long day at the office. His shyness is belied by his all white feathers and soft yellow beak; Pekin is brave enough to tell you the truth, but in a voice soft enough to calm you down. The wild one

Mallard is the bad boy. The one your parents told you to stay away from. But doesn't that just increase his appeal? He's also the duck in the flock that's way older than the others for some reason.
