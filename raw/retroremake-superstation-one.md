## The world's first affordable FPGA gaming console. A recreation of the best console of the 90s. Open source from day 1.

##### SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ is our attempt to make an affordable mainstream FPGA gaming console. It comes packed with all of the features that you need, including multiple video output options, multiple USB ports, built-in Wi-Fi & Bluetooth, and support for original controllers and peripherals.

## 
                We don't believe in locking down current hardware to sell *future* hardware.
              

SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ is designed to play PS1 games, but the hardware fully supports the MiSTer FPGA platform. If you want to play games from another system, simply load up a core. All without needing to wait for a jailbreak.

Click here to learn more about available MiSTer FPGA cores.

## Built-in support for modern TVs & CRTs

It's important for a retro gaming console to support retro hardware. SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ features multiple video output options, as well as support for simultaneous digital and analog video output.

## We bring the party, you bring the games.

SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ does not ship with any built-in games, or copyright material. The base system supports playing backups of PS1 games and ROMs for other systems via the included Micro SD, or via a USB drive. Disk support and additional IO can be found in the SuperDock.
