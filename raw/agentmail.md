AgentMail gives AI agents their own functional email inboxes, the same way Gmail gives humans one. It's an API-first platform built specifically for two-way email communication in agentic workflows, not just one-way notification sending like Mailgun or SendGrid.

The core idea is simple: your agent needs to receive a 2FA code, parse an invoice, or reply to a support thread. AgentMail handles the inbox infrastructure so you don't have to bolt email onto a system that wasn't designed for it.

**Key capabilities:**

Common use cases include browser agents that extract OTP codes during signups, scheduling assistants that manage calendars over email, document processors that ingest attachments, and customer service agents that route support emails. Any agent that needs to communicate over email fits the pattern.

AgentMail is infrastructure for agents that own their own inboxes from the start. It's SOC 2 compliant and built on redundant multi-region infrastructure, which matters when agents are running autonomously and can't tolerate missed messages.
