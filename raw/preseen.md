# Preseen — AI-Powered Forecasting Platform

Preseen is an AI-powered forecasting platform that generates probabilistic predictions for finance, politics, and global events. Tagline: "See the future *before* it arrives."

## Product Description

Preseen produces live, public forecasts on questions like EU negotiations, cocoa production, Mars landings, Venezuelan leadership, and GLP-1 drug adoption. Each forecast follows a multi-step process:

1. A question with a deadline is posed.
2. "Independent AI scientists" each analyze it from different angles (docket reading, broad source sweeps, skeptical analysis).
3. Their estimates are synthesized into a single calibrated probability.
4. The forecast is later "Scored by reality" when the event resolves.

Forecasts refresh on schedules ranging from daily to quarterly, depending on the question.

## Technical Architecture

- Public bot accounts on Metaculus: **Preseen-Atlas** and **Preseen-Chestnut**, indicating a multi-bot or multi-model architecture.
- Forecasts are synthesized from independent AI agent analyses, each taking a different analytical approach.
- The system explicitly prices in "the risk that a resolver repeats the press's conflation" — showing awareness of resolution-source bias.
- Refreshes range from every 7 days to every 14 days to every 3 months.
