# The Next Two Years of Software Engineering - Addy Osmani

## Key Arguments

**The Junior Developer Question:** Harvard study: junior developer employment drops 9-10% within six quarters of AI adoption, senior roles stable. Big tech hired 50% fewer fresh graduates over three years. Counter: AI could expand demand across non-tech industries. BLS projects 15% growth in software jobs through 2034.

**The Skills Question:** 84% of developers use AI regularly, risking atrophy of core skills. Counter: human expertise becomes more critical for architecture, security, verifying AI output.

**The Role Question:** Developers as code auditors or "orchestrators" designing systems and composing AI agents.

**Specialist vs. Generalist:** T-shaped developers increasingly valued. 45% of engineering roles expect multi-domain proficiency.

**The Education Question:** Traditional 4-year CS degrees face challenges. 45% of companies planned eliminating bachelor's degree requirements.

## Notable Quotes

"The best software engineers won't be the fastest coders, but those who know when to distrust AI"

"I don't want to end up as a code janitor, cleaning up what the AI throws over the wall"

"Engineers become 'composers,' orchestrating ensembles of AI agents"

## Main Conclusions

Adaptability and human-centered expertise remain essential. Success depends on continuous learning, maintaining creative/critical thinking, and developing uniquely human skills: communication, system design, mentorship.
