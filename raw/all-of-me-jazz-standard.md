# All Of Me — Jazz Guitar Lesson

Comprehensive jazz guitar tutorial covering melody, harmonic analysis, chord-scale choices, and a transcribed solo over the 1931 Simmons & Marks standard "All of Me."

## Melody

The melody is built from clear, singable phrases moving mostly stepwise — easy to memorize and good for developing feel and phrasing.

## Harmonic Analysis

- **Form:** 32 bars, ABAC structure (two identical A sections)
- **Key:** C major (Real Book version); original 1931 recording is in Bb major
- **Harmonic movement:** Largely built on secondary dominants and circle of fifths movement

### Chord-by-Chord Scale Choices

| Chord | Function | Recommended Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Cmaj7 (bars 1-2) | Tonic (I) | C major / C major bebop (major + b6) |
| E7 (bars 3-4, 9-10, 19-20) | Secondary dominant (V/VI) | A harmonic minor (= E Phrygian dominant) |
| A7 (bars 5-6, 21-22, 28, 31) | Secondary dominant → Dm7 | D harmonic minor (= A Phrygian dominant) |
| D7 (bars 13-14) | Secondary dominant (V/V → G7 → C) | D Mixolydian |
| Fm7 (bar 26) | Modal interchange — iv minor | F melodic minor |
| Diatonic chords of C | I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi | C major scale |

## Bebop Scales

- **Major bebop scale:** Major scale + b6 (for major chords)
- **Dominant bebop scale:** Mixolydian + natural 7 (for dominant chords)
- **Minor bebop scale:** Dorian + natural 7 (for minor chords)

## Solo

Includes a transcribed single-note solo built from arpeggios and bebop scales. Forum discussions extend the analysis into Barry Harris-style scale outlining (substituting G7 over E7, tritone subs like Bb7 over E7).
