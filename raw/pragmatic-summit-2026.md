# The Pragmatic Summit 2026

Inaugural one-day curated conference for senior engineers and engineering leaders, hosted by Gergely Orosz, author of *The Pragmatic Engineer* newsletter. Held February 11, 2026 in San Francisco. Application-based admission, ~400 attendees, $499. Single-track, 8am–6pm.

Sponsored by Statsig and Linear. 28 speakers.

## Main Stage Sessions

### How AI is Reshaping the Craft of Building Software
A grounded conversation on how AI coding tools are impacting developer workflows, and what building software with AI agents will look like for product and engineering teams.
- **Vijaye Raji**, CTO of Applications, OpenAI
- **Tibo Sottiaux**, Head of Engineering, Codex
- **Gergely Orosz**, Author, The Pragmatic Engineer

### Data vs. Hype: How Orgs Actually Win with AI
An in-depth look at how software engineering teams are using AI coding tools today. What the data reveals about adoption versus transformation, and why outcomes vary so widely across companies.
- **Laura Tacho**, CTO, DX

### Building World-Class Engineering Orgs in the Age of AI
A fireside chat on what "AI-native" orgs look like in practice: how teams are structured, how roles blur across PM/design/engineering, and what changes when the bottleneck shifts from writing code to intent, review, and verification.
- **Rajeev Rajan**, CTO, Atlassian
- **Thomas Dohmke**, Co-Founder & CEO, Entire
- **Gergely Orosz**, Author, The Pragmatic Engineer

### Closing Thoughts
Thomas Dohmke (former CEO of GitHub) founded Entire.io to build the world's next developer platform. In this exclusive first, Thomas talks about his company, team, and plans — and Gergely closes the summit.
- **Thomas Dohmke**, Co-Founder & CEO, Entire
- **Gergely Orosz**, Author, The Pragmatic Engineer
