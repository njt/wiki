# The Pragmatic Summit 2026

The Pragmatic Summit is a one-day, curated conference for senior engineers and engineering leaders, hosted by Gergely Orosz, author of *The Pragmatic Engineer* newsletter. The inaugural event was held February 11, 2026 in San Francisco.

## Format

- 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM PST, single-track
- ~400 attendees, application-based
- $499 per ticket
- Target audience: Senior SWEs, Staff Engineers, Tech Leads, Principal Engineers, EMs, Directors of Engineering, VPs of Engineering, CTOs

## About the Organizer

Gergely Orosz writes *The Pragmatic Engineer*, one of the most widely read newsletters on software engineering practice. He previously worked at Uber, Skype, and Microsoft. The summit is an extension of the newsletter's perspective: practical, high-signal, aimed at senior practitioners who build and lead.
