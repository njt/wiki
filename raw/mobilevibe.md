## What is mobile vibe coding?

Vibe coding from your phone, on the real machine your code already lives on.

Vibe coding is when you describe the change and let an AI agent ship it — you're directing, the agent's typing. **Mobile vibe coding** is doing that from your phone, with the agent running on your real desktop where your files, tools, and AI editors already live.

No cloud sandbox. No remote desktop streaming. No copy-pasting between devices. Just your AI agent on the computer you already trust, doing real work, controlled from anywhere you happen to be.

MobileVibe is the app built around mobile vibe coding. Bring your own agent — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf) — and your phone becomes the steering wheel for the machine that ships your work.
