# BookOrbit

Your reading space.

A self-hosted home for your ebooks, audiobooks, comics, and PDFs.

Your files stay on your hardware, and every device stays in sync.

## Features

- Docker deploymentOne Compose file, local storage, no subscription.
- Multi-format readersBuilt-in readers for ebooks, PDF, comics, and audiobooks.
- Multi-user + OIDC/SSOPer-user libraries, permissions, and provider login.
- Powerful metadata9 providers, bulk editing, and field-level rules.
- Kobo + KOReader syncTwo-way progress sync with Kobo and KOReader.
- Book DockStage uploads, review metadata, then finalize.
- OPDS catalogPrivate feed for KOReader, Thorium, Moon+, and more.
- Reading analyticsCharts and widgets for library and reading insights.
