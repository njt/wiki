   Is AI adoption transforming the
   way your enterprise works?
   Buying tools alone is not transformation. Effective system-wide
   diffusion is. A tool licensed and rolled out to 100,000
   employees does not change how the work gets done. What
   changes the work is the operating system around the tool – the
   rituals, the redesign, the measurement, the manager behavior,
   the learning loops, the culture of experimentation. That is what
   we mean by a diffusion engine, and it is a key determinant of
   whether AI translates into measurable business value.
