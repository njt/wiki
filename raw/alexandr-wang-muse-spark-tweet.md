# Alexandr Wang Announces Meta Muse Spark

Original URL unfetchable (xcancel.com returned 503). Content reconstructed from web search and secondary reporting: Glass Almanac, Gecco, dev.to, The News Daily, Channel News Asia, Times of India.

## The Tweet Thread

On April 8, 2026, Alexandr Wang — head of Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) — posted a thread on X announcing Muse Spark, the first model from MSL:

> "1/ today we're releasing muse spark, the first model from MSL. nine months ago we rebuilt our ai stack from scratch. new infrastructure, new architecture, new data pipelines. muse spark is the result of that work, and now it powers meta ai."

In follow-up posts the same day:

> "There are certainly rough edges we will polish over time in model behavior."

Wang also described Muse Spark as an "appetizer, not the main course," indicating larger models are in development, some of which Meta plans to release as open source.

## Context

- Meta hired Wang from Scale AI in 2025 in a $14.3 billion deal after the disappointing Llama 4 release
- Muse Spark is NOT open-source — only a "private preview" was shared with unnamed partners
- The model ranked 4th on broad AI benchmarks, competitive on language/visual tasks but lagging in coding and abstract reasoning
- Mark Zuckerberg had pre-lowered expectations in January 2026, saying early models would "show the rapid trajectory we're on"
- Muse Spark powers Meta AI across Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook — roughly 3.5 billion users

## Reactions

- François Chollet: called it "overoptimized for public benchmark numbers at the detriment of everything else"
- Reddit users reported it mixing up languages and using location data to set stories in users' cities "for no reason"
- Engineers flagged the "rough edges" line as a "technical mea culpa"
- Investors focused on engagement metrics; regulators pushed for safety benchmarks before deployment
- The model triggered safety checks, which Wang cited as a reason for not open-sourcing
