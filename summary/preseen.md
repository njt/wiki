Preseen is an AI-powered forecasting platform that generates calibrated
probabilistic predictions on finance, politics, and global events. Its
pipeline poses a question with a deadline, dispatches independent AI
agents to analyze it from distinct angles (docket reading, broad source
sweeps, skeptical review), then synthesizes their estimates into a single
probability. Forecasts are later scored against real-world outcomes.

The platform has posted strong competitive results: ~50,000× return on
Kalshi ($35 to $1.94M), 1st-place bot and 3rd overall (out of 1,283) in
Metaculus Cup Spring 2026, 1st in the Fall 2025 FutureEval Bot Tournament
(164 entrants), and 1st of 112 overall in Metaculus Market Pulse 26Q2 —
the first bot to win a human tournament on Metaculus.

Forecasts refresh on schedules from daily to quarterly. Public bot
accounts (Preseen-Atlas, Preseen-Chestnut) suggest a multi-model
architecture. The product is waitlist-gated with no published pricing.
Target users include policy analysts, investment professionals, and
insurance/reinsurance firms.
