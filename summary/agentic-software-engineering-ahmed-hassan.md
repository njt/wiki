# Agentic Software Engineering

Full text extracted from PDF via pdftotext. 14,395 lines. Book by Ahmed E. Hassan (2026), subtitled "Building Trustworthy Software with Stochastic Teammates at Unprecedented Scale." First Edition, v0.5a.

## Overview

A comprehensive framework for making agentic software engineering a mature engineering discipline rather than a prompt habit. Hassan has spent 25+ years in "intelligent software engineering" and works with leaders across large companies integrating AI into software engineering organizations. The book targets technology leaders (CTOs, VPs, directors, staff/principal engineers, tech leads) but includes role-specific guidance for developers, business leaders, educators, and researchers.

Core thesis: **Agentic Software Engineering is the discipline of producing high-quality, reliable, trustworthy software from stochastic contributors, both AI and human, by making the full SE system ready across four pillars: actors, process, artifacts, tools.** It is not about finding perfect agents. It is about engineering reliability on top of components that can fail.

## Key Frameworks

- **SE 1.0 → SE 2.0 → SE 3.0**: From human-driven loops through AI copilots to AI teammates as actors
- **Four Pillars**: Actors, Process, Artifacts, Tools
- **Six Agentic SE Artifacts**: Mission Brief, Mentorship Pack, Workflow Runbook, Consultation Request Pack, Merge-Readiness Pack, Resolution Record
- **Three BOMs**: SBOM (what shipped), BBOM (how built), DBOM (how decided)
- **Four Trust Disciplines**: Delegation, Safety, Accountability, Compliance
- **Four AI Strength Clusters**: Tireless/Non-Judgmental, Strong Communicator, Wide World Knowledge, Zero Replication Cost
- **SE4Humans vs SE4Agents**: Two modalities requiring separate optimized workbenches
