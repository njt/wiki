Article by Siddhi Sundar introducing the concepts of the "Creative Firewall" and "Trojan Prompts" — the boundary between imitation and intention in AI-assisted creative work, and deeply personal prompts so rooted in lived experience that AI could never generate them independently.

## Core Concepts

**Creative Firewall™** — The invisible line separating prompts engineered for optimal output from those carrying genuine creative authenticity. It's both protective and generative.

**Trojan Prompts™** — Prompts that carry something hidden inside: your creative authenticity. Named after the Trojan Horse as "a symbol of creative subversion." These are so personal, memory-laden, and emotion-filled that AI could not independently generate them.

**Five Characteristics of Trojan Prompts:**
1. Anchored in the Senses, Beyond Sight
2. Infuses Raw Emotion or Internal State
3. Draws from Personal Memory or Specificity
4. Embraces Juxtaposition or Tension
5. Implies the 'Why' Without Explaining It

## Matrix of Prompt Types

- **Trojan Prompts** — emerge from memory/creative voice; "shape the machine without being shaped by it"
- **AI Imitations** — mirror what the model has seen; optimized but rarely original
- **Human-Guided Expansions** — fuse personal signals with technical scaffolding

## What the Machine Does With a Trojan Prompt

1. Reaches Deeper Into Latent Space — produces unexpected fusions
2. Outputs With Uncertainty and Possibility — dreamlike transitions, time loops
3. Reveals the Edges of the Frame — poetic, unstable responses
4. Produces Work That Can't Be Easily Replicated

## Key Quotes
