# QLS Classic: Ben Vereen

Episode 232 of Questlove Supreme. Host: Ahmir "Questlove" Thompson. Guest: Ben Vereen, joined by his daughter Kabara Vereen.

## Description

In 2024, Ben Vereen appeared on the show with his daughter. Questlove described it as "one of the most powerful QLS episodes in the show's history." The conversation started with Vereen's "legendary, award-winning career in theater, film, and television" but "organically went someplace much deeper." Vereen discussed "discoveries about his identity" and "traces his knowledge of self" while talking about Roots, Sweet Charity, and "his unforgettable appearance on The Fresh Prince Of Bel-Air." The episode is described as "a powder keg of emotions, realizations, and timely calls for history and truth." Vereen is noted as "finishing his memoir."
