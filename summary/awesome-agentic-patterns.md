# Awesome Agentic Patterns

Curated catalogue of 169+ repeatable, production-ready patterns for autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents. "Tutorials show toy demos. Real products hide the messy bits."

## Pattern Categories (8 Major Areas)

1. **Context & Memory** — 20 patterns: window management, episodic memory, state externalization
2. **Feedback Loops** — 14 patterns: CI integration, self-critique, iterative refinement
3. **Learning & Adaptation** — 7 patterns: reinforcement fine-tuning, skill libraries
4. **Orchestration & Control** — 51 patterns: task decomposition, multi-agent coordination
5. **Reliability & Eval** — 21 patterns: guardrails, observability, reproducibility
6. **Security & Safety** — 15 patterns: sandboxing, access control, audit trails
7. **Tool Use & Environment** — 25 patterns: API design, shell integration, sandbox design
8. **UX & Collaboration** — 16 patterns: human-in-the-loop workflows, transparency

## Pattern Criteria
- Repeatable (multiple teams using it)
- Agent-centric (improves sensing, reasoning, or action)
- Traceable (backed by public references)
