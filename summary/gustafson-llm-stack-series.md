Nick Gustafson's series walks the full modern LLM stack, bottom to top: from
the linear algebra inside a single attention head through training, inference
engines, retrieval, and the agent protocols expected in 2026. It spans ten arcs
and roughly eighty posts, ordered by dependency rather than topic popularity.

The author wrote it as a learning exercise in collaboration with Claude Opus
4.8 and describes it as "thinking out loud with a model" — not an authoritative
reference. He advises readers to double-check anything they rely on.

The ten arcs cover mathematical prerequisites (vectors, softmax, cross-entropy,
gradients, optimizers, GPU numerics), pre-transformer language modeling,
tokenization, transformers from first principles, decoding and inference
algorithms, inference serving systems, training and post-training, evaluation
methodology, retrieval and context engineering, and finally tools, protocols,
and agent loops. The series also includes curated learning paths for
understanding attention, diagnosing inference latency, building with RAG, and
building agents.

The series is a full first draft, with polish and corrections in progress.
