CrossPoint is free, open-source reading software for compact e-readers, "built by the people who read on them." The site is a feature tour of the reader: EPUB typography (justified text, language-aware hyphenation, working footnotes, drop caps, small caps), full control over typeface, margins, and spacing, a font builder for converting fonts you own (or loading TrueType directly on readers with external RAM), an SD-card library browser with search and sorting, and multiple ingest paths — browser, Calibre, OPDS/OPDS 2.0 catalogs, or the reader's own Wi-Fi hotspot.

The reading experience gets its own tour: highlights, clippings, named bookmarks, Quick Resume, KOReader sync for position across devices, offline StarDict dictionaries, community translations and right-to-left text (Indic scripts not yet supported), a "Focus Reading" guided-pace mode, tilt/tap/swipe page turns on supported hardware, and Bluetooth page turners with remappable short and long presses.

Distribution and extensibility are community-shaped: SD card plugins add book catalogs and download sources without reflashing firmware, web tools manage books in the browser and shrink EPUBs with an optimizer, updates come over Wi-Fi free on supported devices (USB for BOOX Picco), and there's a public roadmap with nightly builds. The project funds development through shop links and asks for bug reports, translations, and code. Sync extends outward: reading stats in the companion app, clippings as Markdown or images, and integration with Hardcover, Readwise, BookFusion, and Audiobookshelf.
