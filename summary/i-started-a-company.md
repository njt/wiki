Jesse Vincent announces he founded Prime Radiant, an AI company incorporated shortly before Christmas 2025. A first coworker joined the week of this post (January 13, 2026). Vincent is "not quite ready to talk about exactly which AI stuff" they're working on but describes substantial foundational work: writing job postings, navigating hiring amid "a torrent of agentic job applications," and building infrastructure. He anticipates continuing to release the kinds of tools and ideas he's published over the past year, citing infrastructure needs for the future product. The company site at primeradiant.com is described as sparse. The tone is early-stage momentum: "right now, there's just a ton of stuff to do."

## Key quotes

> "I started a company! ... We're calling it Prime Radiant."

> "I'm not quite ready to talk about exactly which AI stuff we're at work on."

> "I need to figure out how to hire folks in this crazy modern world where any job posting will attract an unending torrent of agentic job applications."

> "Right now, there's just a ton of stuff to do."
