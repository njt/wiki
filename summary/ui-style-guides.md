# UI style guides

Marcin Wichary (of Shapeshift/ex-Figma) has assembled an archive of **69 preserved UI style guides** — "interface guidelines" — from the early 1980s to today, hosted on aresluna.org. It is a link-and-mirror collection: for each platform he gives a one-line gloss and links to scanned or mirrored guidelines documents, with lightly cleaned-up mirrors (better image pixellation, inlined images, fixed YouTube embeds) and a downloadable archive of everything.

The coverage is a potted history of GUI design authority: Commodore Amiga's Workbench, GEM/TOS on the Atari ST, OSF/Motif and CDE, IBM's CUA, OPEN LOOK (Sun and AT&T), NeXTSTEP and OpenStep, SGI's Indigo Magic, RISC OS, Taligent, and the classic Apple lineage from Lisa through the Macintosh HIG to Aqua and today's Liquid Glass-era guidelines. Mobile and handheld platforms get their own entries: Palm OS and webOS, Newton, Magic Cap (General Magic), PenPoint, BlackBerry across four generations, Nokia's S60 and Symbian, Windows Mobile and Windows Phone 7, Android from 2012's Design through Material v1 and v2, and iOS from the skeuomorphic iPhone HIG through iPadOS. There are also application-specific guides (Microsoft Office's ribbon) and Java's Look and Feel guidelines.

Wichary annotates with a light, knowing hand: GNOME's guideline versioning is "very confusing," Windows has "a little hole here around 1990, where Microsoft was preoccupied with CUA framework in partnership with IBM," webOS today runs "smart TVs, refrigerators, and projectors." The collection credits other maintainers whose collections contributed — Geof Crowl, Matt Sephton, Matthias Rauterberg, Přemysl Eric Janouch — and invites missing additions.
