Century Library's "Antique Fonts" is a browsable collection page for restored typefaces — 92 items described as "scanned, cleaned up, and converted into a working font" from original specimen plates in the archive. The collection is explicitly not a modern redesign: "What you see is what was once printed – charming imperfections and all," with character sets limited to what survives in the source material.

The listing shows roughly fifty named fonts across two pages, mostly OTF files dated between April 2025 and March 2026: Victorian ornamental drop caps, Fraktur and blackletter display faces, copperplate and Spencerian scripts, distressed serif "type specimens," and woodcut-style block letters. A few entries admit to being "modern remake[s]" of antique families, a small tension with the restoration rhetoric.

The page is heavily instrumented — WooCommerce shop plumbing, moodboard features, member paywalling ("the best pieces in the archive are for members"), and pixel trackers for Facebook, Google Ads, Pinterest, and, notably, an OpenAI pixel. Downloads are metered "since May 2026"; the site claims 1.44M total downloads across its 50,000+ asset library.
