# performative-ui

A satirical React component library by Nathaniel J. Smith (vorpus) mocking the visual tropes of AI/startup landing pages. 27 components, MIT licensed.

Tagline: "Components that signal how oversubscribed your funding round is."

## Components

### Atoms

- **Sparkle** — "Add ✦ to any noun to ship it twice as fast."
- **GradientText** — "When italic isn't billion-dollar enough."
- **StatusDot** — "Always green, even when it's not."

### Primitives

- **Button** — "We made the button move so you'd click the button."
- **EyebrowPill** — "Where the model name goes when there's nothing else to say."
- **Prompt** — "The textarea every AI builder ships instead of explaining what their product does."

### Banners

- **StickyBanner** — "Funding news disguised as utility."

### Heroes

- **Rotator** — "Because saying 'everything' wasn't ambitious enough."
- **WordRoll** — "All the breadth-flexing of a Rotator, without making the visitor wait for it to type."
- **PromptHero** — "We replaced the value prop with a text input."
- **AsciiHero** — "For hackers, by people who follow the right newsletters."

### Backgrounds

- **Aurora** — "Three blobs and a generation defined."
- **NodeGraphBackground** — "A neural network, conceptually."
- **FloatingSparkles** — "Magic doesn't ship itself."

### Surfaces

- **GlassCard** — "Backdrop-filter: ambition."
- **MockIDE** — "Real code is coming. This is the trailer."

### Conversation

- **ChatBubble** — "If it's in a bubble, it must be true."
- **TokenStream** — "Server-sent events (SSE) were added to the HTML5 spec in 2008 but never used until 2025."
- **ChatFAB** — "There's no escape now."

### Social Proof

- **LogoMarquee** — "Trusted by everyone you've heard of, including the ones that didn't sign."
- **LogoRow** — "Static logos are for when you only have six."
- **StatCounter** — "Numbers that go up are better than numbers that don't."
- **CommunityBadge** — "Stars are the new MAU."

### Pricing & Conversion

- **PricingCard** — "The middle one is glowing. Choose accordingly."
- **BeforeAfter** — "On the left: chaos. On the right: us."
- **WaitlistForm** — "Demand we manufactured ourselves."
- **Popover** — "Built for conversion, not consent."

Now generally available. `npm install performative-ui`
