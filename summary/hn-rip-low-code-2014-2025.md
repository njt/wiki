## Article Summary

Zack Liscio argues AI-powered coding poses an existential threat to low-code platforms. The fundamental ROI calculus flipped: "the cost of shipping code now approaches zero." His firm ditched Retool for AI-built internal tools in "just a couple of sprints" and hasn't looked back. The build-vs-buy calculation now tilts toward building with AI.

## HN Discussion Summary

157 comments. The discussion systematically dismantles and reconstructs the article's thesis. Key dimensions: whether low-code is dying or merging with AI, the maintenance burden of AI-generated code, MS Access nostalgia as the gold standard of citizen development, and the gap between "shipping code" and "shipping systems."
