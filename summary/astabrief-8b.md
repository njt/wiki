# AstaBrief-8B (model card)

Ai2's open-weight (Apache 2.0) Qwen3-8B fine-tune that turns a research question plus retrieved literature excerpts into a cited report. It is a distilled system: the SFT/DPO pipeline is trained on reports generated by frontier models — Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet, o3, o4-mini, GPT-4.1, DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1 — from both multi-step Asta ScholarQA and single-step pipelines.

The DPO stage uses AstaBrief_DPO_Mix: real user queries, two candidate reports per query, winners picked by GPT-4.1 and DeepSeek-R1 judges, with only judge-agreed pairs kept after validating 95% judge–human agreement. Training ran in open-instruct on 8×H100 (7 epochs, DPO-Norm, β=10, 16k seq).

Benchmarks (ScholarQA-CS2 test): 87.0 average, beating base Qwen3-8B (77.3) and the SFT checkpoint (83.7), with citation recall up from 64.6 to 78.2. Against full systems, AstaBrief-8B scores 86.3/87.0 on SQA-CS2 dev/test versus Asta ScholarQA's 87.6/86.2, and wins 72% of head-to-head comparisons on the test set — a single 8B forward pass competitive with a multi-agent research pipeline.

Usage requires the exact fine-tuning prompt format; deviating degrades behavior. Intended for research and educational use.
