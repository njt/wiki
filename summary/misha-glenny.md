# Misha Glenny

Michael V. E. "Misha" Glenny (born 25 April 1958) is an English journalist, author, and broadcaster specializing in southeast Europe, global organized crime, and cybersecurity.

## Biography

Born in Kensington, London. Son of Juliet Mary Crum and Michael Glenny, a Russian studies academic and translator. Educated at Magdalen College School, Oxford; University of Bristol (Drama and German); Charles University, Prague. Speaks English, German, Serbo-Croat, Czech, and Portuguese.

## Career

- Central Europe correspondent for The Guardian and BBC, specializing in the Yugoslav Wars
- 1993: Won Sony special award at the Radio Academy Awards for "outstanding contribution to broadcasting"
- 2008: Published *McMafia: A Journey Through the Global Criminal Underworld*, arguing international organized crime could account for 15% of global GDP
- Post-2008: Advised US and European governments on policy; ran an NGO aiding reconstruction in Serbia, Macedonia, and Kosovo
- 2011: Appeared in documentary *Raw Opium: Pain, Pleasure, Profits*; published *DarkMarket: Cyberthieves, Cybercops and You*
- January 2012: Visiting professor at Columbia University's Harriman Institute, teaching "crime in transition"
- 2015: Published *Nemesis: One Man and the Battle for Rio* about Brazilian drug trafficker Antônio Francisco Bonfim Lopes ("Nem")
- Executive producer of BBC One's *McMafia* drama series (8 episodes)
- Producer and writer of BBC Radio 4 series *How to Invent a Country* (2011–2019)
- 2019: Presented podcast *Putin: Prisoner of Power*
- 2022: Presented BBC Radio 4 series *The Scramble for Rare Earths*
- May 2022 – March 2026: Rector of the Institute for Human Sciences (IWM) in Vienna
- December 2025: Announced as new host of BBC Radio 4's *In Our Time*, succeeding Melvyn Bragg

## Publications

- *The Rebirth of History: Eastern Europe in the Age of Democracy* (1991)
- *The Fall of Yugoslavia: The Third Balkan War* (1992, revised 1996)
- *The Balkans: Nationalism, War and the Great Powers, 1804–1999* (1999, revised 2012)
- *McMafia: A Journey Through the Global Criminal Underworld* (2008)
- *DarkMarket: Cyberthieves, Cybercops and You* (2011)
- *Nemesis: One Man and the Battle for Rio* (2015)

## Personal

Spouse: Kirsty Lang, English journalist and broadcaster. Three children; daughter Sasha died by suicide in 2014 at age 22. Lives in Shepherd's Bush, west London.
