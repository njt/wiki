# Pocket TTS
By Kyutai

A 100M parameter text-to-speech model with voice cloning abilities that can run on your laptop's CPU.

## Key Details
- 100M parameters -- extremely lightweight for TTS
- Voice cloning capabilities
- Runs on CPU (no GPU required)
- Developed by Kyutai, an AI research lab funded by Iliad Group, CMA CGM Group, and Schmidt Sciences
