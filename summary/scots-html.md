A one-page novelty reference that translates HTTP status codes into Scots dialect. Each numeric code keeps its standard meaning but gets a Glaswegian voice: 200 becomes "NAE BOTHER", 404 becomes "NO FUCKIN HERE PAL", 418 becomes "AH'M A FUCKIN TEAPOT", 500 becomes "AH'VE FUCKED IT", and 502 becomes "THE OTHER BAMPOT'S BROKEN".

The translations are not just swear-word swaps — many of them are genuinely good glosses of the semantics. 401 as "WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU", 402 as "GIE'S YER MONEY", 429 as "SLOW DOON", 504 as "THE BASTARD'S NO ANSWERIN", and 508 as "ROUND AN ROUND YA DAFT TWAT" (infinite redirect loop) each capture what the code actually communicates to a developer, in the register of someone shouting it across a room. The page is a table of code plus phrase, no prose, and includes codes from 100 through 511.
