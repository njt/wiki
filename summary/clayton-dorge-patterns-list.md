Clayton Dorge read Christopher Alexander's *A Pattern Language* in full and
summarized every one of its 253 design patterns on Twitter
(@apatterntolearn). This page collects all those summaries in one place,
grouped into the book's three tiers:

- **Patterns 1–94: Towns** — regional and neighborhood-scale patterns, from
  independent regions and the distribution of towns down to bus stops, food
  stands, and public squares.
- **Patterns 95–204: Buildings** — the shape of individual buildings and
  their interior spaces: entrances, courtyards, room layouts, balconies,
  gardens, and the relationships between public and private zones.
- **Patterns 205–253: Construction** — material and structural detail:
  columns, vaults, doors, windows, light, color, ornament, and the physical
  craft of making spaces.

Each pattern entry is a single-sentence distillation of Alexander's intent.
The page functions as a quick-reference index; the pattern names link to
their original Twitter threads for anyone who wants the fuller treatment.
