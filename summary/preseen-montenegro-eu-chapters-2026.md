A Preseen forecast estimating how many of the EU's 33 negotiating
chapters Montenegro will have provisionally closed by December 31, 2026.

As of July 19, 2026, Montenegro had opened all 33 chapters and
provisionally closed 18. The median forecast is **28** chapters by
year-end, with a P10–P90 range of 22–33 and a mean of 27.5. The
forecasters give an 11% chance of closing all 33 and an 11% chance of
22 or fewer.

The biggest constraint is **Croatia**, which has blocked Chapter 31
over bilateral disputes and is holding up Chapter 14 over cabotage
concerns. Croatia's PM has tied progress to preconditions on multiple
unresolved issues: missing persons, war crimes prosecution, property
rights, and border talks. The rule-of-law chapters (23 and 24) remain
sensitive, and Chapter 27 (environment) has only 38% of its action
plan implemented.

Positive signals include the Commission adopting a Chapter 33 financial
package, treaty-drafting working groups convening, and Montenegro's
2026–2027 accession program covering 581 acts across all chapters. The
forecast acknowledges that even without closing every chapter by
year-end, Montenegro could still join the EU by 2028.

The forecast draws an analogy to Croatia, which closed 11 chapters in
2010 to reach 28 by December of that year before concluding
negotiations in mid-2011 — suggesting fast endgames are possible but
arguing against 33 as a base case.
