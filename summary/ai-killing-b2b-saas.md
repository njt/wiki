# AI is Killing B2B SaaS

## Main Argument
Vibe coding tools threaten traditional B2B SaaS by enabling customers to build custom solutions. But the real threat isn't AI -- it's SaaS companies that fail to adapt.

## The Threat
- Vibe-coded solutions provide immediate value for basic needs
- Customers expect customization and flexibility
- SaaS valuations declining (HubSpot, Klaviyo down ~30%)
- Series E CEO cancelled $30,000 annual renewal after rebuilding with GitHub + Notion APIs

## Why Vibe-Coded Apps Fail
Lack foundational architecture for:
- Security (encryption, XSS, API key management)
- Compliance (SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA)
- Robustness (audit logs, session management, rate limiting)

Real examples: "a finance team storing unencrypted reports in a public S3 bucket," "a sales ops team creating a commission calculator that anyone with the URL could access."

## Survival Strategies
1. Become a system of record -- embed deeply into customer operations
2. Prioritize security & compliance -- communicate invisible but critical value
3. Enable customization -- let customers adapt your platform

## Conclusion
"The survivors won't be the SaaS companies with the best features. They'll be the ones who become platforms."

"AI isn't killing B2B SaaS. It's killing B2B SaaS that refuses to evolve."
