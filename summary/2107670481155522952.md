# Singing on Two DGX Sparks

A one-line X post (339.5K views, Oct 7 2026) from Gary Lau sharing a video "Made on two DGX Sparks using MiniMax H3.1" — an AI-generated singing clip whose punchline is that the singers are the people who made RAM so expensive, singing "Uptown Funk."

## What it is

A joke, but a load-bearing one: the caption is a reproducibility note. It says the whole clip — a MiniMax H3.1 video generation — ran on two NVIDIA DGX Spark desktop boxes, i.e. consumer-adjacent, desk-side hardware, not a cloud cluster. The joke about memory-chip makers pricing the very machines hobbyists need is the frame; the demonstration is the point.

## Why it matters

- **Video generation on a desk.** H3.1-class video generation running locally on two small DGX Spark units is a marker for how far open-weight models plus edge hardware have come — see [[Local and Open Source Inference]].
- **The two-box pattern.** Paired Sparks, not one big machine, as the home-cluster unit for heavy generative workloads.
- **RAM price gallows humour.** The meme itself documents the hobbyist grievance: DRAM pricing is now a gating factor for local inference rigs.
