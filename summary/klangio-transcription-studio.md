# Klangio Transcription Studio

Browser-based AI transcription service that converts songs with multiple instruments into sheet music, MIDI, and guitar TABs. Made in Germany. Over 4 million transcriptions completed.

## How It Works

1. Upload audio file, record, or paste YouTube link
2. AI identifies notes of multiple instruments playing simultaneously
3. Sheet music generated per instrument; review/edit in browser, export in multiple formats

## Three Modes

- **Classic Mode:** Full ensembles of string and wind instruments, piano, guitar. Classical, acoustic, orchestral.
- **Rock Mode:** Multiple guitars, bass, and drums in separate tracks; handles distortion.
- **Universal Mode:** All-purpose for virtually any song or genre.

## Instruments Detected

Vocals, Piano, Drums, Bass, Guitar, Strings, Synthesizer, Wind (Brass, Reed, Pipe)

## Features

- Transcription Wizard for first-use flow
- Free demo: first 20 seconds of any song, unlimited repeats
- Multi-instrumental transcription (polyphonic + rhythmic detection)
- Browser-based Edit Mode (notes, time signature, tempo, key)
- Export: PDF sheet music, quantized/unquantized MIDI, MusicXML, LilyPond, GuitarPro
- Instrument-specific notation (guitar/bass TABs, vocal lyrics)
- Personal Songbook (account-based)
