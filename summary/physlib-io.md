Physlib is an open-source, community project to formalize theoretical physics in Lean 4 — "digitalizing physics." The site is a short landing page stating the project's scope and pointing to its contributions.

The most distinctive element is its contribution-report model: core work by individuals (reviewing, formalization, foundations, refactoring, maintenance, documentation) can be published in **signed, verifiable reports**. Credit for the unglamorous work of maintaining a formal library is made explicit, attributable, and checkable rather than invisible.

That's the whole page — a project card, not an essay — but the verifiable-reports idea is an interesting answer to the classic open-source problem that maintenance and review are unpaid and uncredited.
