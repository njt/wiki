Makefaster.dev is a product landing page for an "AI skill" that runs an autonomous research loop to continuously improve website performance. Installed via `npx makefaster`, it claims to discover performance issues unique to your site, generate and test hypotheses in a safe isolated environment, apply the best improvements automatically, and repeat — "always getting faster."

The headline numbers are averages from a public leaderboard of 199 sites: LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) down 59% and TTI (Time to Interactive) down 49%, measured on cold load where a site has both metrics. The approach is seeded by "learnings from 200 Fable max thinking runs" performed on the top 200 starred GitHub repos with frontends — the idea being that performance knowledge distilled from prior runs on popular codebases transfers to new sites.

The page is thin — a four-step loop diagram, a terminal screenshot, and aggregate statistics — but its shape is notable: performance optimization packaged not as a one-time audit or a tool, but as a continuously running autonomous agent with write access to your site, evidenced by a public leaderboard that doubles as marketing.
