Lumide is a Flutter IDE built with Flutter and Dart itself, avoiding Electron entirely
for what its creators describe as a native-desktop feel. Its standout feature is an
"agentic sidebar" that bakes AI assistance directly into the editor chrome.

The project claims 200ms cold start and 80MB idle memory usage. The macOS download
is around 40MB. It's available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

The landing page is light on detail — no documentation, pricing, or deeper technical
specifics — presenting it primarily as a fast, AI-native alternative for Flutter
developers.
