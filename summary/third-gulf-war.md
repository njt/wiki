# Third Gulf War Research Wiki

## Purpose & Core Function

A collaborative knowledge base documenting a hypothetical 2026 US-Iran war and its cascading geopolitical, economic, and military consequences. Functions as an LLM-powered hypothesis interrogation machine for near-real-time strategic analysis.

## Four Hidden Variables (P1-P4)

1. **P1 TACO Clock**: Trump's political/psychological tolerance as the binding constraint on war duration
2. **P2 Interceptor Race**: Two-clock competition between Iranian launcher attrition and allied interceptor depletion
3. **P3 Infrastructure Strike Intent**: Whether Iran executes concentrated strikes against Gulf energy/water infrastructure
4. **P4 Economic Transmission**: How war effects propagate to households via energy, sanctions, fertilizer, and macroeconomic compounding

## Three Content Layers

1. **Narratives** (synthesized arguments integrating multiple threads)
2. **Hypotheses** (precise, time-stamped claims with confidence levels and validation tracking)
3. **Trends** (structural patterns across military, energy, trade, food, financial, and political domains)
