Apertus 1.5 is a continued pretraining of the Apertus 1.0 models, released
jointly by the ETH and EPFL AI Centers. It ships at 8B and 70B parameter scales,
with the 8B receiving 4T additional tokens of text and multimodal data and the
70B receiving 2T.

The models add native image understanding (documents, diagrams, photos), a
switchable thinking mode for reasoning-heavy tasks, a 262K-token context window
(4× the prior generation), improved instruction-following, and better tool-use
training. They are described as "fully open": open weights, open data, open
values aligned to the Apertus Charter, and full training details.

A technical report with benchmarks, training pipelines, and intermediate
checkpoints was promised in the coming weeks. Model cards and running
instructions are on Hugging Face for both sizes. The announcement also solicits
community involvement through forums, in-person SME events, engineering
positions, and a newsletter.
