# When Life Gives You Lemons, Write Better Error Messages

## Overview
Jenni Nadler documents Wix's comprehensive effort to overhaul thousands of error messages across their platform, calling it "Errorgate 2021."

## Bad Error Messages Share Four Problems
- Inappropriate tone that undermines user trust during critical moments
- Technical jargon that confuses rather than clarifies
- Blame-shifting that shames users or makes the company appear unprofessional
- Generic messaging when specific solutions are available

## Good Error Messages Include
- Clear explanation of what happened and why
- Reassurance about unaffected elements
- Empathetic language without over-apologizing
- Actionable solutions or contact options
- Clear pathways to resolution

## Notable Quotes

"Generic errors are the result of bad development and product. We must all care about it together." -- CEO Avishai Abrahami

"Write it like you're talking to a friend"

The team discovered they were "more like that friend who loves to gossip, but doesn't pick up the phone when life gets hard."

## Implementation
Wix identified 7,643 error-related content pieces, mapped them in code, and prioritized fixes based on frequency and user impact. Used Monday.com boards for tracking across PMs, developers, and writers.

## Conclusions
Error messaging requires cross-functional collaboration rather than isolated content work. Organizations must view error handling as part of the product lifecycle, establish shared responsibility, and empower writers to challenge generic solutions.
