A Hacker News poster noticed a bitcoin donation address in Neovim's site footer and checked the public blockchain ledger. The address holds a single 10 BTC donation from 2023 — roughly $800,000 at October 2026 prices — and the project's last outbound transaction from that address was in 2019, meaning the team has demonstrably held the keys since then but apparently hasn't moved or spent this windfall.

The post is a short open question aimed at the project: does anyone on the Neovim team know this money is sitting there? The poster, a daily Neovim user, hopes it "can come to good use." The verifiable public ledger link is included.

It is a snapshot of an odd corner of open source funding: a project whose upkeep is famously volunteer-sustained, sitting on a crypto donation worth more than most of its annual sponsorship income, untouched because nobody may have looked, or because spending crypto is administratively awkward for a foundation-less volunteer project.
