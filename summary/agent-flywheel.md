## Feature Breakdown

- **One-liner Install**: A single curl command. "No manual configuration, no dependency hell."
- **Three AI Agents**: Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI "all configured with optimal settings for coding."
- **Idempotent & Safe**: Rerunnable, phases resume on failure, SHA256-verified installers.
- **Vibe Mode**: Described as "Passwordless sudo with dangerous flags enabled for maximum velocity on throwaway VPS environments."
- **Modern Shell**: zsh + oh-my-zsh + powerlevel10k with lsd, atuin, fzf, zoxide.
- **Interactive Tutorial**: Running `onboard` after setup gives guided lessons "from Linux basics to full agentic workflows."

## "Why a VPS?" Section

Three arguments:

- **Not Your Laptop**: "Each agent uses ~2GB RAM. With 10+ agents, you need 48-64GB." Running locally drains battery and slows everything.
- **Not AWS/GCP/Azure**: "A 64GB VPS costs ~$40-56/month flat. Equivalent cloud resources would cost 3-5x more."
- **Works While You Sleep**: "AI agents can refactor, test, and iterate autonomously—compounding progress overnight."

## "Is This For You?" — Honest Assessment

**For you if:**
- You want AI writing "real, production code" — "Full implementations, not just suggestions"
- You find sites like Lovable.dev too limiting
- You're willing to invest roughly $500/month in subscriptions
- You can follow step-by-step instructions — "No coding experience required, just patience"

**Not for you if:**
- You want a completely free solution
- You only want occasional AI help with snippets
- You're looking for mobile-first development
- You need "enterprise compliance out of the box"

## About Section

Jeffrey Emanuel states he built this because he was "being inundated with requests from friends, older relatives, and strangers on the internet" asking for help getting started with AI for software development. He wanted a single resource covering everything "from soup to nuts" even for people with "almost no computer expertise, just motivation and desire."

He originally built the open-source agentic coding tools for himself to move faster in consulting work with Private Equity and Hedge Funds.
