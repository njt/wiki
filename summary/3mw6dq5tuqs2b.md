A short Bluesky post from Morgan Davie recommending a "tremendous" new episode of the Origin Story podcast on John Rawls's theory of justice (linked YouTube episode). The hook that makes it timely for Davie: the episode's section on the limits of using a "free speech" lens to think about political donations, which he maps directly onto the #nzpol drama then unfolding around the New Zealand Taxpayers Union.

The post is thin on argument — it is a recommendation with a twist of wry political commentary ("If only, eh.") — but it is a useful signal: Rawls's framework (justice as fairness, the veil of ignorance, and the priority of liberty) being applied as a live lens on campaign-finance and speech debates in New Zealand politics, rather than treated as settled academic history.
