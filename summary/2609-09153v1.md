The paper proposes the **Procedural Graph (PG)**, an explicit, editable directed graph that stores an LLM agent's *procedural* knowledge — the same move a knowledge graph makes for factual knowledge, but for the "what to do next" question. Where a knowledge graph stores (entity, relation, entity) triplets, a PG stores (procedure, relation, procedure) triplets: nodes abstract tool calls, reasoning steps, and states, while edges encode admissible transitions, each annotated with three textual attributes — *condition*, *guidance*, and *pitfalls* (when the transition applies, how to proceed, what to avoid).

The graph is used in two phases. **Online**, the framework localizes the agent's active node from its trajectory, and a guidance model reads the surrounding subgraph (the localized node's neighborhood up to a few hops) and translates its attributes into step-level situational guidance that is appended to the solver's prompt — steering the next action without dictating it. **Offline**, a self-evolution loop improves the graph itself: an LLM refiner contrasts failed trajectories against successful ones, proposes edits (add/delete nodes and edges, revise attributes), and a validation gate on a held-out set accepts only edits that match or improve performance, while rejected candidates are logged as rejection memory to discourage repeats.

Across seven benchmarks (HotpotQA, MultiChallenge, GDPval, ALFWorld, τ-bench, BFCL v3, EnterpriseArena) and four LLMs (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.5 Flash, Grok 4.1 Fast), PG ranks first or joint-first in 21 of 24 model–benchmark settings against seven memory-based baselines (Vanilla ReAct, MemoryBank, RAP, ExpeL, AutoGuide, AWM, KnowAgent). Self-evolution from a minimal skeleton produces graphs that match or surpass hand-designed ones, and the loop can also repair a flawed expert prior that initially hurts performance. On EnterpriseArena's long-horizon CFO simulation, the evolved graph teaches *anticipatory fundraising* — requesting capital months before a liquidity crisis because delivery lags one to six months — raising survival substantially for three of four models. An efficiency study favors localized subgraph guidance over full-graph raw injection or full-graph generative guidance.
