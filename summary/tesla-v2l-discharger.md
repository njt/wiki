# Tesla V2L Discharger

A portable Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) adapter sold by New Zealand company Drive EV that turns Tesla vehicles into 5KW mobile generators via the CCS charge port. Priced at NZ$3,095 (sale) / NZ$3,249 (regular).

## Product Overview

The Tesla V2L Discharger taps into a Tesla's high-voltage battery pack through the CCS port and converts DC current to household AC power (220V) via an internal inverter. It provides two sockets (one 15A, one 10A) delivering up to 5KW total. Marketed for off-grid camping, job sites, and emergency backup power during outages.

## How It Works

The device uses three internal 18650 batteries to initiate operation — these bootstrap the system, after which the inverter takes over, drawing from the car's high-voltage pack. A USB port allows firmware updates if Tesla software changes break compatibility, and also charges the internal batteries if the device sits unused.

**Startup procedure:** Press button → red indicator lights → ~10 seconds → yellow (ready) → plug into car → green flashing (communication) → solid green + LCD on = ready for appliances.

**Shutdown:** Disconnect loads → hold button 2 seconds → blue flashing → unplug → yellow light appears → wait 30 seconds for full shutdown.

## Compatibility and Limitations

- **Vehicles:** All Tesla Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X with CCS functionality. Includes Japanese imports with Drive EV's CCS conversion adapter. Designed exclusively for Teslas — use on other vehicles "could cause damage."
- **Battery range:** Only works when vehicle battery is between 20% and 95%. Won't discharge at 100% or below 20%.
- **Scheduled charging must be disabled** or the device won't work.
- **Weather:** Do not use in rain. IP22 rating (protection against dripping water only).
- **Ventilation required:** Don't block air outlets or fan.
