# SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ — Product Page

The SuperStationᵒⁿᵉ is billed as "the world's first affordable FPGA gaming console" — a recreation of the "best console of the 90s" (the original PlayStation) using an open-source FPGA platform. Made by Retro Remake (HK) Ltd.

## Tagline
"The world's first affordable FPGA gaming console. A recreation of the best console of the 90s. Open source from day 1."

## Description
An attempt to build an affordable, mainstream FPGA gaming console. It includes multiple video output options, USB ports, built-in Wi-Fi & Bluetooth, and support for original controllers and peripherals. It plays PS1 games natively but fully supports the MiSTer FPGA platform for other systems. The company states they "don't believe in locking down current hardware to sell future hardware."

No built-in games or copyrighted material ship with the system — it supports PS1 backups and ROMs via Micro SD or USB drive.

## Specifications

### Hardware
- **FPGA:** Cyclone V
- **RAM:** 128MB BGA SDRAM
- **Video DAC:** 24-Bit ADV7125
- **Connectivity:** Built-in Wi-Fi/BT

- **Cooling:** Supports active/passive cooling
