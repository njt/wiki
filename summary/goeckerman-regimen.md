# The Goeckerman Regimen for Psoriasis

Detailed protocol for Goeckerman therapy: UVB phototherapy + crude coal tar (CCT). First published 1925, remains highly effective.

Efficacy: UCSF study showed 100% of patients achieved PASI75 (75% improvement) by 3 months, vs 67-68% for biologics.

Safety: "Excellent in comparison to oral systemic therapies such as cyclosporine, methotrexate or biologics." Avoids cancer and serious infection risks. "Clinical studies examining patients on CCT for an extended period of time have not shown an increased risk of skin cancer."

Remission: Average 9.5 months to over a year.

Synergistic effect: Tar is a photosensitizer, enhancing UVB response.

Protocol:
1. Evaluation (history, medications, photosensitivity)
2. Cool Down (topical corticosteroids + plastic wrap, 3-14 days)
3. Daily UVB + tar application (2% CCT initially, titrate to 5-10%, occlude 4+ hours)
4. Daily assessment (monitor burning and itching)
5. Maintenance (outpatient phototherapy 3x/week tapering to 1x/week)

Timeline: Decreased scaling after 10 days, plaque flattening after 20 treatments, complete clearance after 30. Typical course 18-24 days for 90% clearing.

Safe for elderly, children, immunosuppressed. Mild side effects (folliculitis, phototoxic reactions). Patients repeat yearly or longer as needed.

Adaptable for eczema (lower tar concentrations, max 5% CCT).
