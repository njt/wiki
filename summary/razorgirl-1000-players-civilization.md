## Video Context

"1000 Players Simulate Civilization: Rich & Poor" by ish (Alex Kisiel), published July 11, 2025. Runtime ~154 minutes. 34M+ views.

1,000 Minecraft players split across two islands: Island 2 ("Rich") — lush, resource-rich, no hostile mobs. Island 1 ("Poor") — barren wasteland, volcano, boiling water, one single tree shared among 500 players. Goal: simulate global economic inequality.

Unscripted but players selected for roleplay commitment. Emergent storytelling: distinct cultures, alliances, betrayals, a central betrayal plot (Fluixon assassinating and framing Saparata), a player who only communicated in rat squeaks. Widely called "the best YouTube video of 2025" and "better than the actual Minecraft movie."
