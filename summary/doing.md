# Doing: Voice Transcription Tool

Mac-native voice transcription, $49 one-time purchase. No cloud processing.

"Your voice never leaves your Mac. No audio uploaded. No cloud transcription. No account." Uses NVIDIA's Parakeet model on-device.

Performance: 60 seconds of audio transcribed in ~400 milliseconds locally ("150x realtime").

Features:
- Real-time transcription with visual cursor indicator
- Screenshot capture during recording
- Audio ducking (automatic volume reduction during recording)
- YOLO Mode (automatic return key press after pasting)
- Customizable Skills for post-processing text

"Stop editing, stop polishing, just talk. LLMs know what you mean, even when your words aren't perfect."

"Own your tools, stop renting." Contrasts against $8-15/month subscription competitors.
